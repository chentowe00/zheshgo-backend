Privacy

Everything stays in your browser

This demo has no accounts, no cookies, no trackers and no backend. Here's exactly what gets stored where.

Saved routes & placesYour My Trip list — browser localStorage only. Export or clear it from My Trip.
Activity logWhich features you used (e.g. opened a guide, saved a route). Stored locally, plus anonymized aggregate counts (feature name only — no personal data, no content) are sent to our server to learn what's useful. See it at the activity log page; clear it there anytime.
Consult formYour submission goes to our human team and is used only to reply to you. Fields are optional and limited to what's needed (dates, group, interests, contact). We never see or store payment credentials or full ID documents. If the server can't be reached, the note is kept in your browser only.
Community draftQuestions you post in the demo stay local and are labeled as such.
PhotosThe photo guide never opens your camera and nothing is uploaded — it uses preset sample images.
What we never ask forPasswords, payment codes, passport or ID numbers, health records. If anything ever asks for these, treat it as suspicious.

AI travel questions

Questions you submit to the AI assistant are sent over HTTPS to ZheshGo's Hong Kong backend and Alibaba Cloud Qwen to generate a reply. Do not include passport numbers, payment details, passwords or other sensitive personal information. The App photo guide still uses sample images and does not upload photos for AI recognition.
