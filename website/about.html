About

Guides you can check

ZheshGo is an early demo for a simple idea: international visitors to Shanghai and Zhejiang deserve practical, honest travel help — not manufactured urgency.

What we are (and aren't)

We're a travel-guide and helper tool — not an OTA, not a booking platform, not a tour operator. We don't sell anything, hold no inventory, and take no bookings. We publish routes, practical notes and answers; you book with official channels yourself, or ask us for human help and we'll point honestly.

Content rules we won't break

  • Unverified facts are flagged. Train times, opening hours, tickets and entry rules carry a “please verify before travel” marker unless we state a checked date. Right now, effectively everything is demo seed content.
  • No fake social proof. No invented reviews, ratings, booking counts, discounts or “only 2 left”. Any illustrative content is labeled as a demo sample.
  • No invented supply. We don't claim teams, guides, vehicles, partnerships or cases we don't have.
  • No prices we can't check. Budget guidance is by cost composition (“rail + tickets + meals”), never made-up figures.

AI disclosure

The travel assistant checks its server connection when you open it and labels live AI availability. When available, Qwen runs on our Hong Kong backend. If the configured service is unavailable, the assistant shows an error rather than a simulated answer. The App photo guide still uses sample images and simulated recognition. AI suggestions are guidance, never booked services.

Images

All artwork on this site is original illustrative SVG created for the demo — abstract Jiangnan motifs, not photographs, not sourced from elsewhere. When real photography arrives, it will be credited properly.
