App feature · photo guide

Stuck at a machine? Point and tap.

Photograph a ticket machine, locker, ordering kiosk, menu or station sign — get English step-by-step help, one step at a time. This demo uses sample images and a simulated matcher: nothing is uploaded, nothing is real vision AI. A real camera-recognition version is the app's next stage.

Simulated recognition — demonstration only

In the demo you pick a facility and use a preset sample image. The interface flow (scan → match → one step at a time → confirm) is what the real feature would look like.

Privacy, when this becomes real

Your photos would be used only to match a facility and show the right guide — never sold, never public, never used for training by default, deletable at any time. We would never ask for passwords, payment codes, passports or full ID numbers.

This feature is available only in the ZheshGo app — the website intentionally does not offer camera or image recognition.
