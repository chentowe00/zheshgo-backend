Questions from the road

A deliberately light corner: real traveler questions and corrections, nothing social-network-shaped. Every thread below is a labeled sample — this is the mechanism demo, not a live community.

How this is meant to work

Travelers post short questions or trip reports; the best answers get folded back into our guides (that's the “experience loop”). No follow buttons, no DMs, no likes inflation, no fake scale.
