Why you can check us

We'd rather say “verify this” than fake certainty.

Unverified facts are flagged

Train times, opening hours, tickets and entry rules carry a “please verify before travel” marker unless we state a checked date. Demo seed content — not field-verified.

No fake social proof

No invented reviews, ratings, booking counts or prices. Anything illustrative is labeled as a demo sample. We'll earn trust the slow way.

Suggestions aren't bookings

AI answers and routes are guidance, not reserved services. When you need a real person, the consult form is there — no pressure, no countdowns.

How we keep content honest

Stuck on a real trip? Ask a human.

For questions the guides and assistant can't settle — dates, groups, accessibility, a route that just doesn't fit — send us a short note. A real person follows up; we never promise unverified prices or availability.
